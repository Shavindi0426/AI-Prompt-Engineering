1. Executive Summary
The customer feedback dataset contains 1,000 customer reviews collected through four different feedback channels. The overall average customer rating is 3.35 out of 5, indicating a moderate level of customer satisfaction.

The sentiment analysis shows that more than half of the customers expressed positive opinions. However, a considerable proportion of customers provided negative or neutral feedback, indicating opportunities for improvement.

The analysis identifies Clothing as the lowest-rated product category, while Beauty & Personal Care receives the highest average rating.

7. Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

1. Improve product quality

Focus particularly on the Clothing category, which has the lowest average rating. Investigate product quality, sizing accuracy, and customer complaints.

2. Address negative customer feedback

Analyze negative reviews to identify recurring problems, such as delivery delays, product defects, poor customer service, and incorrect product descriptions.

3. Convert neutral customers into satisfied customers

Introduce customer engagement activities, loyalty programs, personalized offers, and improved after-sales services.

4. Strengthen customer feedback management

Monitor feedback across social media, websites, email surveys, and mobile applications to identify customer concerns quickly.

5. Maintain high-performing categories

Continue monitoring Beauty & Personal Care and Electronics to maintain their relatively higher satisfaction levels.

6. Monitor customer satisfaction regularly

Use a customer satisfaction dashboard to track average ratings, sentiment distribution, product category performance, and feedback channel usage over time.
8. Conclusion
The analysis of 1,000 customer reviews indicates that the business has a moderate level of customer satisfaction, with an average rating of 3.35 out of 5. Although 50.6% of customers provided positive feedback, 27.6% expressed negative opinions, highlighting areas that require attention.

Beauty & Personal Care is the highest-rated product category, while Clothing has the lowest rating and should be prioritized for improvement. Social Media is the most frequently used feedback channel, making it an important platform for monitoring customer opinions.

Overall, the business should focus on improving product quality, addressing customer complaints, strengthening customer engagement, and continuously monitoring feedback to increase customer satisfaction and customer loyalty.
