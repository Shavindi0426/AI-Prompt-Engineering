4. Key Positive Feedback Patterns
Customers highlighted several positive aspects:
- Product quality: Customers appreciated the quality of clothing, notebooks, storage containers, and other products.
- Packaging: Several customers reported that their products arrived safely and in perfect condition.
- Customer support: Some customers appreciated receiving quick assistance.
- Delivery: Some orders arrived earlier than expected.
- Ordering experience: Customers appreciated the easy ordering process and helpful updates.

5. Major Customer Concerns
The negative feedback reveals several important problems:
1. Poor customer service: Some customers contacted support multiple times without receiving a satisfactory resolution.
2. Delivery delays: Customers experienced deliveries later than promised.
3. Insufficient delivery updates: Customers were not properly informed about delivery progress.
4. Packaging problems: Some products arrived damaged or with missing parts.
5. Misleading product descriptions: Customers received products that did not match their expectations.

6. Actionable Business Recommendations
1. Improve customer support services by providing faster responses and resolving complaints effectively.
2. Strengthen packaging procedures to prevent product damage and missing components.
3. Improve delivery management to ensure orders arrive within the promised time.
4. Provide accurate and detailed product descriptions to manage customer expectations.
5. Introduce a better delivery tracking and notification system to keep customers informed.
7. Overall Conclusion
The analysis indicates that customer satisfaction is moderate, with an overall average rating of 3.03/5. While customers appreciate product quality, packaging, and convenient ordering experiences, significant issues remain in customer service, delivery management, and product accuracy.
The business should prioritize improving customer support, delivery reliability, and product packaging to reduce negative feedback and improve overall customer satisfaction.
